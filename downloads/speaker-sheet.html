About Marc

Marc Alfano grew up in a small town in western Pennsylvania. He graduated first in his class at Le Cordon Bleu and in 2008 worked stages at Restaurant Daniel, Charlie Trotter's and the French Laundry, three Michelin-rated kitchens in one year.

He owned multiple restaurants and founded Christina Maria's Tiramisu with Christina, taking it into seventeen states. Then he went to Bible school. Today Marc and his wife Christina preach and teach in 28 countries. They founded Alfano Ministries International and Marketplace Revival.

He's the author of The Road to Michelin, Faith to Build, GO, Now Faith, Wiser Than AI and the novel The Last Convert.
