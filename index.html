About

Meet Marc and Christina.

Marc started his first business at twenty-one. He graduated first in his class at Le Cordon Bleu, won the Great Chowder Cook-Off in Newport, Rhode Island, owned multiple restaurants, and in 2008 worked stages at Restaurant Daniel, Charlie Trotter's and the French Laundry.

He and Christina founded Christina Maria's Tiramisu and took it into restaurants and grocery stores in seventeen states.

Then he went to Bible school. Today Marc and Christina preach the gospel around the world through crusades, revival meetings and leadership training. They've served together in ministry and business for more than twenty years.

They founded Alfano Ministries International and Marketplace Revival. Marc hosts The 7K Revelation Podcast.
