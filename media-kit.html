Short bio

Marc Alfano is a chef, entrepreneur, evangelist and the host of The 7K Revelation Podcast. He graduated first in his class at Le Cordon Bleu and in 2008 worked in three Michelin-rated kitchens in a single year: Restaurant Daniel, Charlie Trotter's and the French Laundry. He tells that story in his book The Road to Michelin. Today Marc and his wife Christina preach and teach in 28 countries, and they founded Alfano Ministries International and Marketplace Revival.

Full bio

Marc Alfano grew up in a small town in western Pennsylvania, where fine dining meant putting French fries on a salad. He started his first business at twenty-one. In 2002 he enrolled at Le Cordon Bleu in Pittsburgh, graduated first in his class, and opened his own deli.

In 2008 he did something most chefs never get to do once. He worked stages at Restaurant Daniel in New York, Charlie Trotter's in Chicago and the French Laundry in Napa Valley, three Michelin-rated kitchens in one year. He went on to build award-winning restaurants and a national tiramisu brand. His book The Road to Michelin is the map he wishes he'd had, and the lessons work in any industry.

Then Marc graduated from Bible school and went into full-time ministry. He was convinced that business and faith were never meant to be separate. He and his wife Christina have served together in ministry and business for more than twenty years. They preach the gospel in 28 countries through crusades, revival meetings and leadership training, and they founded Alfano Ministries International and Marketplace Revival, which equips everyday believers to carry their faith into business, education, media, government and the arts.

Marc hosts The 7K Revelation Podcast. His books include The Road to Michelin, Faith to Build, GO, Now Faith, Wiser Than AI, The Daily Sword and the novel The Last Convert. Several are published in Spanish, Italian and Polish.
